# Classroom spacecraft reference

Education includes a generic spacecraft inspired by the supplied IM-1 / Odysseus whiteboard photograph, provided on 2026-11-15. These are classroom assumptions, not independently verified IM-1 flight specifications. They do not alter the landing-game physics.

Transcribed parameters: dry mass 1,500 kg; propellant 2,400 kg; full mass 3,900 kg; thrust 12 kN; mass flow 3.88 kg/s; specific impulse 315 s; velocity-change budget about 2,950 m/s; stored energy 4,500 Wh. Reaction wheels use 15 W idle or 180 W active per wheel. Turning acceleration about Y and Z is 0.041 degrees/s² full and 0.106 empty; about X, 0.191 full and 0.496 empty.

Three active wheels consume 540 W. The whiteboard autonomy annotation (apparently 5.6 h) is inconsistent with its energy and power parameters. The educational display uses 8.3 h, from 4,500 Wh / 540 W, excluding other loads, reserves and recharging. Three idle wheels consume 45 W, giving 100 h under the same assumptions. Maximum propellant burn duration is about 619 s, from 2,400 kg / 3.88 kg/s. Power and energy units are kept distinct.

The supplied material does not identify fifteen NASA missions. No unsupported mission catalogue or flight capability claim has been added.
